Your Price

How We Set Your Price

The prices above are where projects start. There is no fixed price list, because every business needs something different.

1

What you want

The features, pages, screens and integrations you need. A bigger or more complex project costs more, a simpler one costs less.

2

What we agree

We talk about your goals and your budget together. You are welcome to negotiate, and we will find a plan that fits you.

3

What the contract says

Everything we agree is written into your proposal and signed contract: the scope, the phases and the final price. That price does not change unless you ask for extra work.
