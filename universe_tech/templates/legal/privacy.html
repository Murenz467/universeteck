Privacy

What we collect and why.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number and company when you request a quote or create an account; the messages, files and approvals you share while we work together; and the date, time and network address when you sign a contract.

How we use it

To answer your request, prepare proposals and contracts, deliver and support your project, send invoices and keep you informed with alerts and emails. We do not sell your data.

Who can see it

Only you and the Universe Tech team working on your project. Client files are never publicly accessible.

Your choices

You can switch email copies off in Profile & settings, update your details at any time, or ask us to delete your account by opening a support ticket.
