Who We Are

One Team for Software, Brand and Hosting

Universe Tech was founded in Kigali with a simple belief: Rwandan businesses deserve the same quality of digital tools as any company in the world, at a price that makes sense for them.

Today we design and build custom software, websites, mobile apps and brand identities, and we host and look after them once they are live. Instead of juggling a developer, a designer and a hosting company, our clients work with one accountable team from the first idea to long-term support.

Every project follows a clear path: a written proposal, a signed agreement, phase-by-phase approval and transparent progress on your own client portal. You always know what is happening, what it costs and what comes next.

Meet the Founder

Murenzi Charles

Founder & Lead Developer, Universe Tech

Charles is a full-stack software developer from Kigali who builds real-world platforms for the Rwandan market. He works mainly with Flutter for mobile apps and Django REST Framework for secure, scalable back-end systems, and he is completing his Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at the Adventist University of Central Africa (AUCA).

He started Universe Tech to give local businesses a dependable technology partner: one that explains things clearly, delivers what it promises and stays around after launch. He leads the team's engineering and works directly with clients to turn their ideas into products people use every day.

Technology should work for the business owner, not the other way around. Our job is to make it simple, secure and worth every franc.Murenzi Charles, Founder
